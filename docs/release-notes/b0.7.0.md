# Travellers b0.7.0

## ⚠️ Read before updating

- **The config has been cleaned up.** Many options were removed or renamed (spawn density and distances, worker caps per village, follow teleport, despawn, merchant and dog options…). Settings you changed in earlier versions may be lost: check your config after the first launch.

## New features

### Horse companions
- Travellers, raiders and henchmen can now show up with a **bonded horse**. The pair stays together for good, including while offscreen, and nobody can steal the horse.
- They fight from the saddle, swim their horse across water (towing it behind a boat if needed), dismount to pass narrow gaps and jump obstacles their horse can clear.
- In villages they leave the horse at the edge and walk in.
- Spawn rate, speed and dismount behaviour are tunable in the `Mounts` category.

### Stable zones
- A new zone type next to the Barracks: create it the same way (sneak + right-click with Millénaire's Summoning Wand).
- Assign a henchman to a Stable from the **Zones** screen: he tames, saddles and breeds horses, and sleeps in the stable at night.
- Send another henchman on a **Get Equipment** order to take a horse from the stable (a group leader outfits the whole group).
- The Stable keeps working while you are far away, and you are notified when a horse is tamed, saddled or a foal is born.

### New henchman menu
- Right-click a henchman for a new tree menu with a live portrait.
- Drag buttons with right-click to create shortcuts.
- Works at any GUI scale. An optional mode keeps several branches open at once (`Worker hub` config).

### Formation Hub reworked
- Right-click on the map or use the new action menu to give orders: go, follow, merge, halt, get equipment, form a group.
- New group **Attitude**: Attack, Defend or Hold Position. (I don't remember if it was already in 0.6.x)

For example, you can select a leader, right-click a controlled village, a barrack or a stable.

### Village visits
- Travellers now stop in villages on their way: they tie up their horse, stroll the streets, visit the tavern, market or temple, read information panels etc.
- A wandering henchman leaves a small gift of building materials when he leaves. (as it always did)
- Tunable in the `VillageVisit` config category.

### Travellers look at you and greet you
- They glance at who they pass, face you when you stay close, and say hello to you and to each other.
- Tunable or removable in the `Gaze` config.

### Doors, gates and walls
- Travellers open doors and fence gates and close them behind them (was problematic)
- They leave and enter walled villages through the gate instead of getting stuck against the wall.

### Elytra flight (WIP)
- Travellers, raiders and henchmen with an Elytra glide over ravines and valleys.
- Escorts take off with you and use firework rockets from their inventory to climb back up.

This feature is a "fun" feature, still not perfect. No improvements since last update.

### AI dialogue (optional)
**Only works if you enable it in `Dialogue` config**
- Right-click a traveller to hold a real conversation, answered in character by an AI model that **you run on your own computer** (Ollama or an OpenAI-compatible local server). Off by default.
- Travellers know their personality, trade, culture, home village, why they are travelling and what is around them. They can remember across conversations (optional).
- Set up from the in-game `AI Dialogue Setup` screen or command `/travellersdebug dialogue setup`

You can use Llama cpp or Ollama, don't forget to pull a model.
I suggest using Gemma or Qwen models.

This feature needs a good GPU, especially VRAM / RAM, check `modelfit.io` to find the model that corresponds to your hardware.

From the tests I did, 12B models are sufficient.

This feature is WIP and was just an idea I had for fun, I won't prioritize this feature.

### Regionalized travel
- Travellers prefer villages of their own culture and villages their home is on good terms with, avoid those at war with it, and are drawn to larger villages. Distance still matters most.
- Raiders do the opposite and head for foreign or hostile regions.
- Tunable in the `Destinations` category.

### Travel improvements
- Travellers now plan a real route with a real itinerary
- Travellers are more aware of the terrain / biome and will prefer avoiding harsh terrain and slopes

### World map
- Fog of discovery and smoother rendering.
- Stables and palfreniers appear on the map.
- Option to highlight loaded chunks on the population map (operators only).

## Other changes
- **Offscreen world on hold** when nobody is connected (default). Optionally let it run slowly instead (`Offscreen` category). Travellers no longer pile up around world spawn.
- **Raiders** now can heal like travellers do.
- **New sounds**: an "afraid" voice, new choir songs, a fourth Norman personality.
- **Declaring a player hostile** now applies to all your henchmen, and so do the relations with villages.
- **Improved** creative / peaceful checks.
- **Improved** travellers groups fight retaliation.
- **Renamed** the Barracks button to Zones.

## Fixes
