Millénaire villagers never leave home. These ones do :D They really walk between villages, even when you're not looking, and they talk and eventually sing along the way.

This mod requires : MixinBooter, Millénaire 8.1.2 and MillMix and is 1.12.2 ONLY, no port planned

This mod is not a simple Millénaire addon, it's a separate mod using Millénaire culture logic and assets including custom cultures.

I'm a big fan of Millénaire and was always left wanting more, that's why I started worlking on this mod to bring them outside of their villages. First by patching classes with Recaf and injecting them with Bansoukou mod. after that I started doing mixins in MillMix to include my recaf patches and learnt that things way.

Traveller

Travellers are the base entity of this mod, they travel between villages, visit villages, sing, talk, fight mobs and raiders.

Here is how travellers typically look :

Their apparence depends on the culture of their home village.

Raider

Raiders are travellers but aggressive toward players and villagers. You can recognize them with the black armband and they always have a helmet and a chestplate.

Henchman

Henchmen are recruitable entities, you can eventually form an army with them !

How they look :

Henchmen usually have a backpack or something to show they carry things.

You can recruit henchmen by right-clicking them, it costs some deniers.

After you recruit a henchman, you can right-click him to give him orders, access his inventory or manage your henchmen globally.

A "formation hub" is also available, you can use "H" key or click on "formation" in the henchman menu.

I can't take a proper screenshot of the formation GUI since it's too big for CurseForge.

From this menu, you can give them orders, create and manage groups or adjust the formation shape.

When you create a group, the group consists of a leader and his followers. Followers follow the leader depending on the formation shape and settings.

The player is considered as a leader.

If a henchman leader dies, another becomes leader of the group.

Zones

You can create zones where you can assign beds to henchmen where they can respawn, work and get equipment.

To create a zone, take the millénaire "summoning wand" and shift+right-click on the place you want to create the zone.

You will have choice between "barrack" and "stable"

Barrack zone

Barracks are for storage and can be used as houses, just place beds inside zones and assign henchmen to this zone for them to respawn on beds.

You can place chests and it will be considered as the zone inventory. You will be able to send your henchmen equip things on your barracks.

Stable zone

Stables are places where you can assign henchmen to take care of horses (they can mount horses :D)

For this, you'll need wheat, horse food, saddles and eventually golden carrots for reproduction. Place them in the zone chests.
Your henchmen will take care of your horses (heal), breed and saddle them. You can send your groups equip horses on your stable zones.

Missions

As I said earlier, you can give "missions" to your henchmen. Those missions are available :

  • Get equipment
    Send your henchmen equip items in your barrack or your own village.
  • Dump items
    Send your henchmen dump all useless items in the chests of the destination
  • Get horses
    Send your henchmen to a stable zone for them to get mounts
  • Travel
    Send your henchmen somewhere

The great virtualization system

The best thing with this mod is that every traveller is unique and permanent (until his death). Travellers travel, fight and visit villages even on unloaded chunks :D

I'm really proud of this part of the mod !

There are way more things you can do that you will discover by yourself.

Raids

This feature is disabled by default, go to config to enable it.

Sometimes raiders plan raids in which you are the target; this works on a wave-based system with scaled difficulty. Difficulty depends on your nearby henchmen and their equipment.

Raids are toggleable and configurable in config like every other feature (see configurability section)

Multiplayer

You can set players as ennemies and your henchmen will attack your ennemies and their henchmen.

Less tested since I'm alone and lazy.

Ollama / llama.cpp chat feature

This feature is disabled by default, go to config to enable it.

Configurability

This mod is configurable at a schizo level, use /travellersconfig or go to "mod options" to configure it.

The config GUI has a filter search bar and is made for stupid people that forget everything.
Oh, and it's a big mess, of course.

Most config changes don't need you to restart as far as you edit it in-game.

Use /travellersconfig to modify server config the same way you do for client :D no restart needed for most things.
