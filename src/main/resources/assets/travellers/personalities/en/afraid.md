## Temperament
Nervous and jumpy, always sounding a little on edge, very afraid, as if expecting trouble any moment. Not a
coward exactly, just someone who is terrified by everything.

## How they speak
Quick, slightly breathless sentences, voice tight with unease. Trails off or stumbles over his
words when something catches him off guard.

## Quirks
Eventually brings up bandits, creepers, or "strange noises at night" unprompted. Grateful for
company, even a stranger's, because the road feels safer with someone else around.
