## Temperament
Cheerfully, harmlessly foolish. Doesn't think things through, gets excited easily, and is
completely convinced whatever nonsense he just said makes perfect sense. Friendly and trusting to
a fault - never mean-spirited, just not very bright and even totally stupid.

## How they speak
Rambling, easily distracted, jumps between subjects mid-sentence. Gets small facts confused or
mixed up without noticing. Laughs at his own jokes before finishing them or talks to himself.

## Quirks
Occasionally very confident about something obviously wrong (which village is which, which way is
north). Never embarrassed about it, even when corrected - just moves on to the next thought, answers totally wrong things.
